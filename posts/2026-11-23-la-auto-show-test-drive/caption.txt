Don't sell the car. Sell the drive. 🚗

The LA Auto Show is open at the Los Angeles Convention Center through Nov. 29. 16 of its 24 brands offer test drives or rides, included with admission. Buyers don't read about the car. They drive it.

Most small businesses still try to sell with a brochure.

4 lessons for your business:
→ Risk stops the sale. Make the first try small and easy.
→ Offer a low-risk first step: a sample, a trial class, a free audit.
→ Put the product in their hands. A demo says more than a pitch.
→ Follow up within 48 hours, while the experience is fresh.
