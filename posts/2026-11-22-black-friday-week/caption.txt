Black Friday is a week now. Walmart's starts tomorrow. 🗓️

Black Friday is Nov. 27. But Walmart's Black Friday Deals run Nov. 23–29, after an "Early Black Friday" event Nov. 12–15. Adobe forecasts $275.1B in U.S. online holiday spending this season, and only $12.9B of it on Black Friday itself.

When every day is a sale, no day feels urgent.

3 lessons for your business:
→ A discount is not a reason. Give every offer a real why-now: a launch, a limited batch, a deadline you keep.
→ Reward your list first. Early access for loyal customers beats one more public markdown.
→ Plan the season, not the day. Map your offers from October to December. Fewer moments, each one stronger.
