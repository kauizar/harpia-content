$88 million. Eight months. Then the Colts walked away. 🦅

In March, Indianapolis gave its quarterback a two-year, $88M deal. On Friday, after a 26-3 loss in Houston, the team released him and named Anthony Richardson Sr. the starter. The numbers had spoken: 13 interceptions in 11 starts and a 3-8 record.

Whatever you think of the football, the business move is rare. Most companies keep funding the wrong bet because they already paid for it.

3 lessons for your business:
→ Set the kill line before you spend. Pick the number (CAC, ROAS, churn) that ends the bet, on day one.
→ Ask the restart question every 30 days: would we start this today? If no, stop funding it.
→ Keep a bench ready. A cheaper test running in the background turns a failure into a promotion, not a panic.
