The Trap

Sunk cost
is not a plan.

In March, the Colts gave their quarterback a two-year, $88 million deal. On Friday, after a 26–3 loss in Houston, they released him and handed the job to Anthony Richardson Sr.

Companies rarely move that fast. They keep paying for the agency, the tool, the campaign, because they already paid for it.

Business takeaway

Money you spent is gone either way. Judge every bet on what it returns next.

The Playbook

Cut fast.
Cut clean.

01

Set the kill line on day one

Write the number that ends the bet (CAC, ROAS, churn) before you spend.

02

Ask the restart question

Every 30 days: would we start this today? If the answer is no, stop funding it.

03

Keep a bench ready

Run a cheaper test in the background. When the main bet fails, you promote, not panic.
