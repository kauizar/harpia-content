The hottest Black Friday deal this year? The boring stuff. 🧴

Adobe data: U.S. online sales of personal hygiene products were up 40% from Nov. 1 to 22 vs. the October average. Household cleaning +33%. Clothing basics +27%.

Shoppers are being "very intentional," as Walmart puts it. From a skincare brand in Santa Monica to a coffee roaster in Silver Lake, the products people rebuy are the ones that sell when wallets are tight.

4 lessons for your business:
→ A treat can wait. A need sells now.
→ Lead with what customers rebuy, not your flashiest product.
→ Sell the stock-up: multipacks and bundles, not just a % off one item.
→ Make the reorder one tap: subscribe-and-save, refill reminders, a saved cart.
