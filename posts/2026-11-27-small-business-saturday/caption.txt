Tomorrow they'll shop small. The real question is whether they come back. 🛍️

Small Business Saturday is Nov. 28. 88% of U.S. shoppers say they're likely to shop at small businesses this holiday season, the highest level since 2012 (American Express Shop Small Impact Study, via the SBA).

From a coffee shop in Silver Lake to a boutique on Abbot Kinney, the traffic will show up. Most of it walks out and never hears from you again.

4 lessons for your business:
→ A first visit is not a customer. Plan for visit two.
→ Capture every buyer: text or email sign-up at checkout, with a small perk.
→ Give a reason to return: a December-only offer that works on the next visit.
→ Follow up within 7 days: a thank-you, a new arrival, a personal invite back.
