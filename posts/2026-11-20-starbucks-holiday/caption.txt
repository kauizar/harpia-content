Starbucks' biggest U.S. sales day ever wasn't Black Friday. It was the day the holiday menu came back. 🦅

On Nov. 5, the holiday drinks returned. CEO Brian Niccol told employees it was the highest single-day sales in the history of its U.S. company-operated stores. More than 1 in 3 drinks sold that day were holiday drinks.

No discount drove that. A ritual did.

3 lessons for your next launch:
→ Own one date. Same week every year turns a release into a tradition.
→ Make it seasonal. Limited menus and small drops give people a reason to buy now.
→ Tease the return. Build the countdown so launch day is already on their calendar.
