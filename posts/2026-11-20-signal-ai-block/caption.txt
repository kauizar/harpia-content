Every tech company is adding AI. Signal just built a wall against it. 🦅

On Nov. 20, Signal said it will block screenshots by default on iOS 27, with a new "Block Siri AI" setting. On Android, its screen security turns on when an AI assistant tries to read the screen. It called built-in AI agents an "existential threat."

That's not a feature update. That's positioning.

3 lessons for your brand:
→ Name what you won't do. Refusing the shortcut your rivals take is a position.
→ Make it the default. A promise buried in settings is a footnote. A default is proof.
→ Explain the tradeoff. Say what it costs and why it's worth it. People trust a clear reason.
