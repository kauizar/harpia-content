Your chatbot needs a human. California just made it law. 🤖

California signed AB 1609 on Sept. 28. Starting Jan. 1, 2027, companies with over $500M in revenue must tell customers when a chatbot is AI, and give them a simple way to reach a real person. The target: a good-faith effort to connect in 15 minutes, or a callback within one business day.

The law hits the giants. But every Los Angeles business is in the middle of its busiest support season right now, and customers will compare.

4 lessons for your business:
→ Let AI handle the easy questions. Never use it as a wall.
→ Say it’s a bot. Customers forgive automation, not tricks.
→ Put “talk to a person” one tap away in every chat and phone menu.
→ Measure minutes to a human. Speed is part of the brand.
