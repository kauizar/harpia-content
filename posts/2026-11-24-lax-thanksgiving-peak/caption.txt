Thanksgiving is the quietest day at LAX. ✈️

LAX expects nearly 2.6 million travelers from Nov. 19 to Nov. 30, up nearly 5% from last year. The busiest day is Sunday, Nov. 29. The second busiest was Friday, Nov. 20, almost a week before the holiday. Thanksgiving Day itself? About 146,000, the lightest of the 12.

Most Los Angeles businesses staff for the date on the calendar. Not for the real rush.

4 lessons for your business:
→ Your peak is in your data, not on the calendar. Pull sales by day and hour.
→ Reservations and preorders show you demand before it arrives.
→ Add people and stock for the spikes. Run lean on the quiet days.
→ The rush often starts earlier than you think. Be ready a week ahead.
