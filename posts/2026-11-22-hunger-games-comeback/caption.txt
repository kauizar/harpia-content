$44M in 2023. $120M this weekend. Same franchise. 🎬

"The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes" opened far below the original Hunger Games films. Lionsgate didn't walk away. This weekend, "Sunrise on the Reaping" opened to $120M in North America and $225M worldwide.

Most businesses would have called the first prequel a failure and moved on.

3 lessons for your business:
→ Split the brand from the launch. A weak debut judges one offer at one moment, not the audience you built.
→ Find the gap before you relaunch. Ask buyers what was missing: price, timing or message. Fix that first.
→ Judge by lifetime, not day one. Track what a launch earns over months, not just the first week.
